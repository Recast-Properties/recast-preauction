The market you sell into

  • Rates jumped. The 30-year averaged 7.28% on October 1, up from 6.43% in early July. The same payment now buys about 8% less house. Every comp on this page went under contract before most of that move. Freddie Mac weekly survey; the 8% is arithmetic.
  • Ellis County is flat and slow. September: 1,067 listings, 442 price cuts during the month, 398 pending against 433 a year ago. Asking price per foot was $193 against $195 a year ago. Realtor.com inventory data.
  • Closed sales through August were steady. 309 sales in the county, down 4% on the year, with 4.6 months of inventory. Across Dallas-Fort Worth, homes closed at 94.8% of their original asking price. NTREIS via MetroTex.
  • You list into the slowest months. The county's median days on market was 71 in September and reached 80 last January. Realtor.com inventory data.
  • Builders are the competition. Lennar reports incentives near 12% of price. Builders hold most of the listings in three of these subdivisions: Christian Meadows, Prairie Ridge and Ridge Crossing. Lennar third-quarter results; Zillow listings, October 2.
  • Flip margins in Dallas are the thinnest of any big metro. A 1.8% gross margin before rehab and carry in the second quarter, against 21.5% nationally. ATTOM; Texas price data is partial.
